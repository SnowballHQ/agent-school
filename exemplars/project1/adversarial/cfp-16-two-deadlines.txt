Annual Symposium on Applied Machine Learning in Healthcare (SAMLH 2027)

SAMLH 2027 will be held at the University Hospital, Heidelberg, Germany, from 12 to 14 April 2027. The symposium is held in person; a virtual attendance option for registrants only is offered but all presenters must attend on site.

We invite full papers (8 pages) and extended abstracts (2 pages). Papers should present original work. Intended for researchers with an advanced background.

Full-paper deadline: 20 November 2026. Extended abstract deadline: 15 January 2027.

There is no honorarium. Student presenters may apply for a travel award of up to 800 EUR.
