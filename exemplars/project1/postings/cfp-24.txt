Postgres Europe Conference 2027 - Call for Presentations

Where: Prague, Czechia. When: 7-9 November 2027 (in person). Our speakers are Postgres contributors and users. We look for talks on query planning, replication, extensions and migration stories. Advanced users are the main audience.

The call for presentations closes 1 June 2027. Accepted speakers get free conference entry; the conference covers hotel for up to 3 nights for talks accepted before 1 August 2027. Travel costs are paid by the speaker. No fee.
