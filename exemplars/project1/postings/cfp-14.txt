Frontend Futures - Call for Papers (2nd call)

Frontend Futures will take place in Amsterdam on 14-15 October 2027. The programme committee invites proposals on web platform features and tooling. The first call closed on 15 September 2026 and we are reopening submissions until 31 January 2027 because of the many requests.

In-person only. All levels. Speakers receive free admission to the conference and the speakers' dinner. We reimburse travel up to 400 EUR.
