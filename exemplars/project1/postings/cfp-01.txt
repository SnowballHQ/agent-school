PyHarbor 2027 - Call for Proposals

PyHarbor is back in Lisbon, Portugal from 18 to 20 March 2027, a three-day in-person conference for Python developers. We are looking for talks of 30 minutes and workshops of 90 minutes. Beginners and experienced speakers are equally welcome; most of our audience has a year or two of Python behind them.

Proposals close on 30 November 2026. Speakers are notified by 15 January 2027.

We cover flights (economy) and two hotel nights for every accepted speaker. We do not pay speaker fees.
