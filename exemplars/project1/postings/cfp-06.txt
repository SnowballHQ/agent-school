Women in Data Science Nairobi - Call for Abstracts

Join us in Nairobi, Kenya on 22 October 2026 for a one-day in-person event. Abstracts for 20-minute talks on data science in healthcare, agriculture and finance are welcome. Beginners particularly encouraged to apply.

Abstracts are due six weeks before the event.

We provide lunch for speakers. Travel costs are not covered, with exceptions for speakers from outside East Africa at the organisers' discretion.
