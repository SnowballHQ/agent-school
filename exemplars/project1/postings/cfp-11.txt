Linux Storage Workshop - call for presentations

The workshop is an invitation-oriented gathering of kernel and storage developers, held in Boston, MA, USA from 2 to 4 November 2026. Presentations are expected to be technical and for experts.

Proposals must be received no later than 25 September 2026. Attendance is in person only. There is no fee for speakers and no stipend.
