DevOps Days Cape Town 2027

DevOpsDays Cape Town is looking for speakers for the 2027 edition. The event takes place in person at the V&A Waterfront, Cape Town, South Africa on 5 and 6 August 2027.

We would like talks on platform engineering, incident response and developer experience. First-time speakers are strongly encouraged.

The CFP closes on 1 April 2027. We offer a travel stipend of up to 600 USD to speakers outside the Western Cape and waive the ticket fee.
