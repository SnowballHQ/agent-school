DataFest 2027 (Track A Zurich, Track B online)

DataFest has two tracks. Track A is an in-person conference in Zurich, Switzerland, 21-22 June 2027. Track B is a live-streamed track with the same dates. Both accept 30-minute talks about analytics engineering and data quality. Intermediate level.

Please submit by 28 February 2027. Zurich speakers receive hotel and train reimbursement; Track B speakers receive a speaker gift. No fees are paid.
